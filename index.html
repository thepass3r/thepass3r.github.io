Kira, a face that stays the same.

The store's brand ambassador is an AI character I designed: her face, body, apartment and even her phone are fixed references. She posts on Threads in her own voice and stars in the reels.

  • Reference-driven videoMiniMax H3 with up to nine reference images: face, room, single garments. The wall and baseboard match to 2-3 px across separately rendered clips.
  • Upscaled in latent spaceDrafted at 576x1024, upscaled to 1088x1920 in a second pass. Fits on the same 16 GB card.
